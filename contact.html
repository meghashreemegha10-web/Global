Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our layouts, approvals, construction billing, and registrations.

What approvals do your plots have?

All our plotted developments are DC Converted for residential usage with sanctioned master blueprints, clear survey sketches, and individual E-Khata issuance ready for registration.

Can I get a bank loan for plots and homes?

Yes. Our legal title files are vetted and approved by leading financial institutions including SBI, HDFC, ICICI Bank, and LIC Housing Finance with up to 75%–80% financing available.

What are construction package rates?

Our residential construction packages start at ₹1,950 / sq.ft for standard vitrified flooring and ₹2,200 / sq.ft for granite flooring, with itemized milestone billing and zero surprise extras.

How do I verify legal documents?

You can review the complete title kit — 30-year EC, mother deeds, DC conversion order, mutation registers — at our JP Nagar office or request a digital copy via WhatsApp before paying any booking fee.

Can you customize our house floor plans?

Absolutely. Our in-house architects work closely with your family to draft customized 2D floor plans, 3D modern elevations, and 100% Vastu-compliant layouts tailored to your exact lifestyle.

How soon can registration take place?

Once booking is completed and legal documentation verified, sale registration at the Sub-Registrar's office can be scheduled in as little as 7 to 10 working days.
