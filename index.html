Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about our residential layouts, construction packages, legal clearances, and site visits.

What services does Global Mahanshi Builders provide?

We provide residential plot development, independent house construction, and villa construction across South Bengaluru — including custom plot + turnkey home construction packages handled under one roof.

What do construction packages start at?

Our standard residential construction packages start from ₹1,950 / sq.ft (vitrified flooring) and ₹2,200 / sq.ft (granite flooring), with complete itemized specifications and stage-wise billing provided before you sign.

Where are your projects located?

Our flagship Sai Roots Layout is in Jigani near Electronic City, and Sri Sai Prasanthi is at Ramasandra, Jigani. Completed landmark communities include Green Valley Phase 1 (Begur) and Druthi Nice City.

How do I book a site visit?

Message us on WhatsApp (+91 96327 34110), call our office, or submit the enquiry form below. Our team provides guided tours with project engineers every day of the week between 9:00 AM and 6:00 PM.
